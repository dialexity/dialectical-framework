## `<broader_journey>`: which positions are asked to refine

**Refinement recursion (`<broader_journey>`, `utils/edge_context.py`):** every generative position in a tetrad is rendered the coarser Transformations its edge descends from and told to be more concrete than them — this is what makes a bigger wheel's Ac+ "find a colleague who could be your friend" where a one-perspective wheel said "find a friend". `build_coarser_context(parents)` renders the ancestry coarsest-first and indented, one `Action:` and one `Reflection:` line per parent (a Transformation carries BOTH spiral directions); `coarser_journey_section(parent_context, refine)` wraps it and returns `""` when there is nothing coarser — deliberately not a "no parents" note, since a layer-1 wheel has no ancestry to be MISSING and saying so invites the model to treat a complete answer as a gap. **THREE instructions, not one, because the two rendered lines are refined by different positions:** `REFINE_ACTION` for Ac+, `REFINE_REFLECTION` for Re+/Re-, `REFINE_TETRAD` for Ac-. Collapsing them is the tempting simplification and it loses the point. `_score_hs` and `_generate_category_reframings` are deliberately NOT asked — HS is a judgement, so nudging it with "be more concrete than the broader path" is a different class of defect, and the reframings derive from positions already refined (transition-level HS does NOT gate; `HS_THRESHOLD` is polarity-level only, and this paragraph said otherwise until 2026-09-11 — the reason not to refine it never depended on that). **The negatives are bound by VALENCE, not concreteness, so no instruction points one at a coarser line:** a parent's Ac-/Re- are deliberately not rendered, so the only coarser lines available are positives, and telling Ac- to be more concrete than the parent's Ac+ asks it to refine the statement it is defined by contradicting. Hence `REFINE_TETRAD` names no position (it points at the whole transition) and `REFINE_REFLECTION` names `Re+` explicitly, because `ReSideCompletionDto` returns Re+ AND Re- from ONE call — **when one call produces two positions, an instruction that names neither is an instruction to both**, and the tidy-up that breaks it ("your reflections refine it") passed every pre-existing test. **The trap, and it is worth carrying past this feature: BEING IN THE CONTEXT WINDOW IS NOT BEING ASKED.** `TransformationGeneration` holds ONE `ConversationFacilitator`, so until 2026-09-11 Re+/Re-, HS and the reframings all read `<broader_journey>` out of the history Ac- had filled — refinement by accident of ordering, with nothing enforcing that Ac- ran first and nothing stopping a future `isolate()`. They were UNASKED, not blind, and a signature table read as "only Ac- is refined" was wrong in the other direction. **Ac+ was the real hole and it is the position the recursion exists FOR:** `ActionExtraction` has its own facilitator, `isolate()`s every insight band, and runs in Phase 1 before any parent lookup happened, so it saw nothing in prompt OR history — and its candidate is copied into the tetrad verbatim, so the tetrad's LEADING position refined nothing however much context the three later calls got. So never conclude a position is refined from its signature, and never from visibility: read the submitted prompt. Measured at k=4 (`tests/probe_transformation_recursion.py`, climb arm, 18 of 20 edges have ancestry): Ac+ asked in 54 of 60 prompts, Ac- 18 of 20, Re 18 of 20, HS and reframings 0 of 20 with 18 history-only — the same ratio three times, which is the part that breaks if any position is dropped. The lookup happens ONCE per edge in `ExploreTransformations._phase1_for_edge` and travels to Phase 2 on `_EdgeProcessingData.parent_context`, which is a net query REDUCTION (Phase 2 runs per CANDIDATE, so its own lookup was three identical questions an edge — 20 lookups for 20 edges now). That field is three-state and the third is load-bearing: a rendered hierarchy / `""` = looked up, no ancestry / `None` = nobody looked, which `TransformationGeneration.resolve` reads as permission to look it up itself; without the `""` a layer-1 wheel would re-query 3x an edge for an answer already settled. Cost is prompt text, not calls (273 against 177 at k=4, unchanged by this). Tests: `tests/test_refinement_context.py` (the three builders, the valence/concreteness boundary, the wiring through the REAL pipeline against a committed Wheel, one-lookup-per-edge, HS staying uninstructed) + the probe's exposure table.

## Scaling: 6N Transformations per wheel

**Scaling:** N-PP wheel = 2N edges, N edge pairs. Opposite-edge Transformations are Ac+/Re+ role-swapped (E1's Ac+ = E3's Re+). **Transformations = 2N × |`INSIGHT_CATEGORIES`| = 6N**, not 2N: `ActionExtraction` returns one Ac+ candidate per insight category (Generative/Configurational/Corrective, `concerns/ac_re_taxonomy.py`) and `ExploreTransformations` Phase 2 generates a tetrad per candidate, so each edge carries 3 depth alternatives (`docs/graph.md` "multiple Transformation alternatives", paper Fig. 8). 1-PP = 2 edges/1 pair/**6** Transformations — measured on a real provider in `tests/test_single_perspective_explore_real_llm.py` (3 × `A → T`, 3 × `T → A`). Cost follows the same multiplier: each Transformation is 4 sequential `TransformationGeneration` calls, + 2 audit calls only when `settings.audit_transformations` is on (default off — see the Settings section).
